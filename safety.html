Safe Night

Nightlife safety on After Dark Society

Discovery should not stop at the door. Every listed room can carry verification, security badges, last-entry time and a one-tap path to emergency services. Plans can be shared with a trusted contact when you book.

How we score a venue

Verified listing

Partner venues confirm licence, hours, cover and a floor manager contact before going live.

On-floor security

CCTV, bag check, metal detection, first-aid and female staff are shown as badges — not assumed.

Exit & ride

Last entry, well-lit exits and cab-stand notes help you plan the last mile before last call.

Before you go out

Share your night plan

Add an emergency number on the booking form. After confirm, you can send venue name, time and maps link to that contact.

Report a concern

Unsafe door policy, harassment or a fake listing can be flagged. After Dark Society reviews partner access; emergencies still go to 112 first.
