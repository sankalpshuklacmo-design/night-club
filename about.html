About After Dark Society

After Dark Society is a nightlife discovery and booking layer for India. We map clubs, DJ nights, live rooms and table inventory so you can decide where the night actually happens — before you leave home.

The product is designed to scale from dozens of rooms in a city like Lucknow to thousands of venues nationwide. Club owners manage hours, events, DJs, offers and bookings from a dedicated dashboard.
